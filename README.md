But then you see the background and text visualizer, with the pixelization parameter you can add a lot of color very quickly. And everywhere you look, even the LFOs you get that pixeled sauce. 


# Burning CD Feature Failure

So basically I had a whole plan of this cool idea where the way music would be played was all through CDs. You'd take mp3s you had, put them all together on a CD, design the CD cover, and then of course add it with all the tracks to your digitial CD collection. And you would've been able to share this with your friends, so you can share them with one another and it would've been fun.

The thing I realized about 5 minutes later though is that although how cool this idea was, actively distributing copyrighted music without the creator's permission is very very illegal. Like not just website being taken down, like you're cooked kind of deal. So I realized I had to shut that down pretty quick even though I was so excited about it.

I knew that I was going to make atleast one CD of my own sort of experimental modular song collections that would be a fun way to show off the CD collection feature, but then I realized this is actually one of the greatest collaboration opportunities of all time.

### Copyright rules going forward

There will be rules and a warning given where basically if you want to make a visualizer public that has music in it that you've imported, it must be copyright free or cleared. I don't want to be infringing if someone decideds to post a bunch of popular music on their visualizers.
